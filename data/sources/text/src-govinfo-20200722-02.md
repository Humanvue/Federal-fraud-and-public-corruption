DAVID SCHWEIKERT, Republican, of Fountain Hills, AZ; born March 3,
1962; education: B.A., Arizona State University, Tempe, AZ, 1988;
M.B.A., Arizona State University, Tempe, AZ, 2005; professional:
business owner of a real estate company; realtor; financial consultant;
member of the Arizona State House of Representatives, 1989-94; member of
the Arizona State Board of Equalization, 1995-2003; former Treasurer,
Maricopa County, AZ, 2004-06; religion:

[[Page 13]]

Catholic; married: Joyce Schweikert; committees: Ways and Means; Joint
Economic Committee; elected to the 112th Congress on November 2, 2010;
reelected to each succeeding Congress.
