DUNCAN HUNTER, Republican, of Lakeside, CA; born in San Diego, CA,
December 7, 1976; education: graduated, Granite Hills High School; B.S.,
business administration, San Diego State University, San Diego, CA,
2001; professional: business analyst; military: captain, U.S. Marine
Corps, 2002-05; U.S. Marine Corps Reserves, 2005-08; religion:
Protestant; married: Margaret; children: Duncan, Elizabeth, and Sarah;
committees: Armed Services; Education and the Workforce; Transportation
and Infrastructure; elected to the 111th Congress on November 4, 2008;
reelected to each succeeding Congress.
