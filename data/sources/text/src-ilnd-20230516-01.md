MINUTE entry before the Honorable Steven C. Seeger as to Daniel Dingle: Change of plea hearing held on May 16, 2023. Defendant enters a plea of guilty to count 1 of the criminal indictment. Plea agreement to follow. The Court finds the defendant competent to enter a guilty plea, and defendant's guilty plea is knowing, voluntary, and supported by an adequate factual basis. The Court accepts the defendant's guilty plea and enters a plea of guilty. This matter is referred to the probation department for the preparation of a presentence investigation report. The Court will hold off on issuing a sentencing schedule pending resolution of the co-defendant matter. The Court will set sentencing hearing and schedule at a later time after the filing of a status report. A status report is due by November 15, 2023. The pretrial dates such as the pretrial conference and trial date are hereby vacated. The parties shall submit a proposed sentencing schedule to the Courtroom Deputy via email.
