ORDER  as to Nadine Menendez: The Court is in receipt of defendant Nadine  Mendendez's letter requesting an extension of time to file a motion pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 2255. (Dkt. No. 958.) Generally, a defendant has one year from the date o n which a judgment of conviction becomes "final" to file a motion pursuant to section 2255. See 28  U.S.C. § 2255(f). A judgment of conviction is not final during the pendency of a direct  appeal. See Clay v. United States, 537 U.S. 52 2, 527 (2003) ("Finality attaches when  [the U.S. Supreme Court] affirms a conviction on the merits on direct review or denies a  petition for a writ of certiorari, or when the time for filing a certiorari petition  expires.") Defendant has  appealed her conviction to the U.S. Court of Appeals for the  Second Circuit, and that appeal remains pending today. (See Dkt. No. 909.) As such, her judgment of conviction is not yet "final," and the one-year period for filing a   motion  under section 2255 has not yet begun to run. For this reason, the Court denies defendant's request for an extension of time to file a motion pursuant to  section 2255 as moot.
